⚡ The 3 Pillars of Every Valid Trade (Must Agree)
1. Location (Context)
Price tests the ±2.0σ VWAP Band (Statistically extended).
2. Aggression (The Trap)
A BigTrade Bubble (≥75 lots) prints on the extreme wick.
3. Proof (Absorption)
Price fails to advance & CVD shows divergence/stall.
🔴 Bearish Absorption (Short Setup) Fade the Highs
  • 1
    Location: Price stretches up and touches the +2.0σ VWAP Band (Upper Band).
  • 2
    Institutional Print: A Green Buy Bubble (≥75) prints at or near the top wick (buyers hitting Ask).
  • 3
    Absorption: Price fails to expand higher. Candle leaves an upper rejection wick.
  • 4
    CVD Check: Sub-panel TheflowX_cvd makes a Lower High (Divergence) or goes flat.
  • 5
    Entry: Enter Market Short on rejection candle close (or Sell Stop 1 tick under candle low).
STOP LOSS 1 to 2 points (4–8 ticks) above the high wick.
TARGET 1 Close 50% at +1.0σ Band & move stop to B/E (+1 tick).
TARGET 2 Close remaining 50% at VWAP Midline (Orange Line).
🟢 Bullish Absorption (Long Setup) Fade the Lows
  • 1
    Location: Price dumps into and tests the -2.0σ VWAP Band (Lower Band).
  • 2
    Institutional Print: A Red Sell Bubble (≥75) prints at or near the bottom wick (sellers hitting Bid).
  • 3
    Absorption: Price fails to expand lower. Candle leaves a bottom rejection wick.
  • 4
    CVD Check: Sub-panel TheflowX_cvd makes a Higher Low (Divergence) or holds baseline.
  • 5
    Entry: Enter Market Long on rejection candle close (or Buy Stop 1 tick above candle high).
STOP LOSS 1 to 2 points (4–8 ticks) below the low wick.
TARGET 1 Close 50% at -1.0σ Band & move stop to B/E (+1 tick).
TARGET 2 Close remaining 50% at VWAP Midline (Orange Line).
☑️ Pre-Execution 5-Second Checklist
🚫 Walk-Away Red Flags (NO TRADE)
✖ No trades in the middle: Never fade between +1σ and -1σ bands.
✖ Trend Breakout: Full-body candles closing outside the 2σ band with no wicks.
✖ News Events: No entries within 2 mins of CPI, FOMC, or NFP.
✖ Missing BigTrade: If no bubble appears, do not guess absorption.
